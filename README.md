# CHIMERA WING: The Epigenome War

A Galaga / U.N. Squadron-style shooter with SNES-style widescreen graphics. You fly the **VX-3 Chimera**, a
transforming fighter, off the deck of a space carrier against the fleets of **Dr. Helena Voss**,
a rogue epigeneticist who has taken over the galaxy's pilots by switching their genes on and off.

## The story

Voss never changed a letter of anyone's DNA. She changed which genes are *used*. Your ally,
**Dr. Mira Kato**, was her lab partner and built the Chimera to stop her. Before every mission Mira
briefs you on a real piece of epigenetics (Waddington's landscape, the Dutch Hunger Winter,
agouti mice, queen bees, X-inactivation, imprinting, Yamanaka's reprogramming and more), tells you
why that planet matters and what its boss is, and Voss answers. Along the way you learn why she's
doing it, in the same order whichever route you fly.

Destroying Voss's base at **The Citadel**, in the heart of Andromeda, plays the epilogue. After that
the **Echo campaign** begins: every system opens again, harder, against the **Voss Echo**, with new science facts, and
you keep your money, level, specials and ship.

## The campaign

The game is an open world of **3 galaxies, 12 star systems and 84 planets**. Your **space carrier**
flies anywhere the star map allows and docks at the orbital station over each planet.

The **star map** has three zoom levels. Pick a galaxy to zoom into its four star systems; pick a system
to zoom into its star and planets (up to ten, circling on their orbits); pick a planet to fly there.
B zooms back out. You can also click or tap galaxies, systems and planets.

| Galaxy | Star systems |
|---|---|
| MILKY WAY | Sol, Alpha Centauri, Barnard's Star, Sirius |
| MAGELLANIC CLOUD | Tarantula, S Doradus, SN 1987A, N44 Bubble |
| ANDROMEDA | NGC 206, Mayall II, M32, M31 Nucleus (Voss's Citadel) |

- **Every planet has 2 to 4 missions.** The last one, the **STRONGHOLD**, opens once you have flown the
  others. Clearing every mission on a planet clears the planet.
  - **PATROL**: a vertical Galaga-style stage.
  - **INTERCEPT**: a side-scrolling stage.
  - **GROUND RAID**: a side-scrolling stage full of tank columns and missile sites.
  - **CONTROL GROUP**: a bonus stage. The planes only take the odd shot; hit all 64.
  - **STRONGHOLD**: two legs and a boss. Either a vertical approach and a side-scrolling assault on a
    grounded **boss warship**, or a side-scrolling approach and a vertical **capital-ship flyover**.
  - **SUPPLY RAID**: a contract on every *cleared* planet, for players short of credits. A 30-second
    side-scrolling run at Voss's convoys hauling SAM (S-adenosylmethionine, the methyl donor her DNMT
    enzymes use). Every unarmed cargo tank you destroy pays (40 CR, plus 15 per system tier). It pays
    in full every time and never counts toward clearing anything.
- You **take off from the carrier** before every leg and **land on it** after.
- **Clear half the planets in a system to secure it.** That opens the systems linked to it. Secure
  every system in a galaxy to open the next galaxy. Clear every planet in a system to liberate it.
- **Difficulty depends on where you fly**, like the zones of an open-world game: later systems and
  outer planets are harder, and each new galaxy's machines are tougher.
- If your ship goes down, the **mission fails** and the carrier brings you back. You keep your money,
  XP and upgrades, and can try again.
- The game **saves** every time the carrier docks. Pick **CONTINUE** on the title screen. Saves from the
  older six-sector version are converted.

Each system has its own boss warship and capital ship, and its own mix of enemies (Splitters in Alpha
Centauri, drone swarms at Barnard's Star, armored planes and tanks further out).

### Guns

Every gun belongs to one form, and each form of a hull takes exactly two: its start gun and one
upgrade. The hulls make a **ladder**: each one starts with the guns the hull before it buys as
upgrades, so buying a hull gives you its three start guns and the only gun to buy is the upgrade. Guns
are owned across hulls, so an upgrade bought for one hull is already yours, at its level, on the next.
The **hangar** opens before every sortie: each gun row switches between the form's two guns (the
upgrade shows `BUY <GUN>` until you own it; the CHIMERA has one gun per form). The hull's strength in a
form multiplies the gun's damage.

**Gun levels are bought, never earned in flight.** Every starbase's MARKET has a GUN LEVELS page for the
guns you own: LV2 to LV5 cost 0.2, 0.3, 0.5 and 0.75 times the gun's price (the CHIMERA's guns from a
base of 1,000). LV4 is sold from the Magellanic Cloud on and LV5 in Andromeda (`NEEDS MAGELLANIC`,
`NEEDS ANDROMEDA`). A level counts on every hull that carries the gun. Market gun rows show the form
(`FTR`, `GRD`, `BTL` in the form's color) and which hulls take the gun, owned ones in white.

## Running it

No install or build step. Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

- `index.html?stage=N` jumps straight into a planet's stronghold for testing: planet `ceil(N/2)` in map
  order, odd N = first leg, even N = the boss leg (e.g. 2 = Earth's base, 4 = the flyover at Mars,
  168 = the Citadel). Add `&hull=2` for a hull, `&passives=drone,rear` for skills and
  `&fit=charge,swivel,rail` for the three forms' guns, `&mod=barrier2` for a module and `&ord=missile2`
  to carry ordnance (test runs own every gun and module, and 6 of each ordnance). Test runs don't save.
- `index.html?touch` turns on the touch controls on a desktop browser.
- `F` toggles fullscreen and `M` mutes the sound. The game menu (Start) turns **SHAKE** (screen shake on
  hits and big blasts) on or off; the setting is remembered.

| Game menu   | Start                   | Enter / P / Esc |

**Touch screens:** on an Android phone (Chrome or Samsung Internet) the touch controls switch on by
themselves (on other touch screens, at the first touch). The game fills the screen, in landscape. In
flight, put your left thumb anywhere on the left half for a floating joystick; **A** fires (hold it),
**B** fires the special, **X** transforms, **START** opens the game menu (from any screen), where
**EXIT GAME** saves, leaves fullscreen and takes you back out of the game. The title screen has
**EXIT GAME** too. In menus and on the star map, tap a row,
card or planet to pick it and tap it again to use it. The mouse works the same way on a desktop.

Controllers use the browser Gamepad API. Xbox and PlayStation pads work as-is. Generic USB pads
(SNES/NES-style) also work, and that includes pads whose D-pad shows up as a "hat switch". If your
buttons land in odd places, use **CONTROLLER SETUP** on the title screen to remap FIRE, SPECIAL,
TRANSFORM, PREV FORM and START. The mapping is saved per controller. Browsers hide a controller
until you press one of its buttons, so press one after plugging it in.

## Pilots

A new game starts by picking who flies the Chimera. Each pilot has three stats. Your hull, its market
upgrades and a trained point every sixth level (3, 9, 15...) add to them, up to 8 weapons, 8 shields and 5 special:

- **Weapons** sets how hard your shots hit: (1 + weapons) / 4 of a gun's damage, so 1 = half, 3 = full,
  8 = 2.25 times. At weapons 1 a MiG takes two hits.
- **Shields** is how many hits the ship can take. When they run out the HUD flashes
  **DANGER**, and one more hit destroys the ship and fails the mission. Shields are repaired at
  every takeoff, and score bonuses (20,000, then every 70,000) repair one.
- **Special** is how many times you can use your special weapon on each leg.

| Pilot    |               | Weapons | Shields | Special | Perk |
|----------|---------------|---------|---------|---------|------|
| MAVERICK | Hotshot ace   | 2       | 2       | 1       | Gun levels cost 25% less |
| TURTLE   | Iron wall     | 1       | 3       | 1       | 10 seconds without a hit recharges a shield (stacks with AUTO REPAIR) |
| DRAC     | Alien tactician | 1     | 2       | 2       | Loads one more rack of ordnance |

## The epigenetics

- **Methylators** (purple flying wings) fly down and fire a methylation beam. If it catches you,
  the form you were in is **silenced** and you can't use it. The Methylator carries it away, like a
  captured ship in Galaga. Shoot that plane down to demethylate the gene and get the form back
  (+1000 points). If your last working form gets silenced, it costs a shield and all your forms come back
  (with no shields left, it destroys the ship).
- **Acetylated** planes (gold) fly faster, fire double shots and are worth double points.
- **TET capsules:** gold planes sometimes drop a green-and-white capsule (more often when a form
  is silenced). Fly into it to demethylate one silenced form. With nothing silenced, it's worth
  1000 points. Real TET enzymes start the process that removes methyl marks from DNA.
- **Bullet colors:** the color says what a bullet does, whoever fires it. **Red** flies straight at
  where you were. **Orange** falls straight back, a little slower (bombers, the heavy's curtains).
  **Yellow** is slow, then bursts into three red shards (the heavy, gold gunships). **Violet** is slow
  and bends toward you for a moment (snipers and the heavy, in the far galaxies).
- **Shot-down bonus:** every leg except control groups and capital-ship flyovers counts the planes
  that came and the ones you shot down (traffic that gets away is missed). The clear screen shows
  SHOT DOWN and pays 100 points per percent plus credits; 100% is PERFECT and pays double. The debrief
  shows the mission's average.
- **Epigenetic memory:** if one form gets 60% or more of your kills in a stage, the next squadron
  adapts and takes half damage from it. Keep transforming.
## The two kinds of stage

When Mira or Voss radios in during a stage, the action **pauses** until you've read it: press A (or B,
or tap) to go on. Held fire doesn't skip it.

- **Vertical** (Galaga-style, top-down, you fly up): four squadrons of 40 planes fly into formation and
  dive at you, one after another. Every form can fly up, down, left and right in the lower part of the
  screen, and the ship banks as it turns. Planes shoot as they fly in, as they dive, and now and then
  from the formation, but each carries only so many rounds, so an old formation goes quiet. On a
  challenging stage they only take the odd shot. Around the squadrons, the leg plays like 1942:
  - **Traffic** flies through and leaves instead of forming up: MiG pairs crossing, vees that break
    away above you, gunships that dive in and cross firing aimed pairs, snipers that park at the top and
    fire bursts until they run dry, and attacks **from behind**, flagged by arrows at the bottom edge.
    Later systems send more of it, and more kinds.
  - Between squadrons comes a 7 to 9 second **intermission**. Past the first few planets, a
    **HEAVY** gunship comes down from the top in one of them (two in the far galaxies): it holds the
    top of the screen, fires fans, falling curtains and later splitting and seeking shells, and leaves
    after 12 seconds. Let it escape and you only lose its 2,000-point bonus.
  - A **red squadron** of five crosses in a line once or twice a leg. Down all five and the last one
    drops a **shield cell** (+1 shield, or 500 points at full shields) and SQUADRON BONUS 1000.
  - A leg runs about 90 to 150 seconds.
- **Capital-ship flyover** (vertical boss): you fight your way in first (two squadrons and traffic),
  then Mira calls it in and a giant Star Destroyer-style ship scrolls beneath you.
  Knock out its gun turrets, fighter hangars and histone reactor cores, and the fighters it
  launches. If you reach the end of the ship with targets left, you come about and fly over it
  again, and again, until every target is gone. The HUD shows the pass and the targets left.
- **Side-scrolling** (U.N. Squadron-style): you face right and every form can fly anywhere
  on the left side of the screen. Every ship here, yours and the enemy's, is drawn in side profile.
  Enemies include:
  - MiG squadrons flying in lines or swooping at you
  - Ambushes from behind, with warning arrows on the left edge first
  - Gunship pairs (later systems) and snipers that park at the right edge (further out still)
  - Slow, tough bombers
  - SAM sites on the ground
  - Methylators that park on the right and fire their silencing beam sideways. If one gets away
    carrying your gene, that form stays silenced for the rest of the leg.
  - Tank columns rolling along the ground, and each system's own enemies

  A side-scrolling assault ends at the system's **boss warship**, a battleship, destroyer or carrier
  grounded on its landing legs. It rolls in along the ground and the scroll stops. Destroy every
  turret, missile launcher, fighter hangar, radar mast and reactor core on it to win. The core stays shielded while the turrets stand (each system's base has its
  own twist on this).

## Special weapons

(every starbase's MARKET has a SPECIALS page; each costs like that hull's upgrade guns and, like them, is

THUNDER LASER, TET BURST, CHRONO FIELD, HYPER MODE and CLUSTER BOMB only come with a hull. After every
briefing, the **hangar** screen fits your guns
(one row per form), the special you'll carry (or ordnance), and the module you've fitted. Fire it with **C** (keyboard) or **B** (controller). Every takeoff rearms it
with as many uses as your **Special** stat.
